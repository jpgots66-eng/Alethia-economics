Stage 03 · The Lesson
The constraint was never capital.
It was the order of decisions.
Same moves. Different sequence. The difference between Severe and Low.
01 · What the founder believed
The constraint was capital.
"We need $400-500K minimum. That's the whole problem." Signal DX scored Decision Architecture as a corroborated strength. The instrument agreed with the founder's self-assessment.
02 · What the advisor read found
The constraint was decision architecture.
Three disasters. Same structure each time. The pattern was named in writing — and pre-answered the founder's exact objection almost word for word before the closing call happened.
03 · What happened next
The engagement didn't proceed. That's the framework working.
The founder chose a different story. Alethia held the diagnostic and moved on. We don't chase fees that require us to pretend the constraint is something it isn't. That discipline is the product.
The Alethia Diagnostic Model
Signal DX gives us the founder's own view — undiluted. The advisor layer catches what the instrument misses. When those two reads diverge sharply, the gap is the diagnosis. That gap is what Alethia is built to close — whether the founder is ready to see it or not.
